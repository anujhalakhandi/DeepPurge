DEEPPURGE

Applied once at the start of boot and again 20 s after boot. Nothing runs in the background. A setting you turn off goes back to stock at the next reboot.

Reset

Puts the system settings the cleaner changed back to stock and turns every tuning setting off. Cleaning can't be undone. Use this before uninstalling the module.

Choose a clean

App logins and data are kept. The cleaner runs only when you press a button here.
